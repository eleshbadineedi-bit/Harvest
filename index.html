Our Story

A farm run by people
who love the land

"We grow food the way our father taught us."

My name's Ramesh Patel, and Green Harvest Farms has been in our family for three generations. What started as a small plot of rice fields has slowly grown into a full working farm — wheat, vegetables, pulses, the works.

We're not a big corporation. It's me, my wife Sunita, our two sons, and a few trusted workers who've been with us for years. We do things the old way where it makes sense, and we've picked up a few modern tricks along the way too.

Our land is in the Anand district of Gujarat. Good soil, decent water access, and neighbors who'll lend a hand when the tractor breaks down — which it does, more often than we'd like.

We sell directly to families, small restaurants, and local markets. No middlemen. That keeps prices fair for you and fair for us.

How We Do Things

Farming the right way —
for the land and for you

We're not certified organic (the paperwork is a nightmare, honestly), but we try to farm responsibly. Less chemicals, more care. Here's what that looks like in practice.

🌱

Reduced Chemical Use

We use pesticides only when we have no other choice. Most of our fields rely on crop rotation and natural pest control to stay healthy.

💧

Drip Irrigation

We installed drip irrigation on about 60 acres a few years back. Uses about 40% less water than flood irrigation and the crops do better too.

🔄

Crop Rotation

We never grow the same thing in the same field two years in a row. Keeps the soil from getting depleted and naturally reduces pests.

🌿

Composting

Crop waste and cow dung from our small dairy go back into the soil as compost. Cuts fertilizer costs and the soil thanks us for it.

☀️

Solar-Powered Pumps

We switched our irrigation pumps to solar two seasons ago. Electricity bills were killing us. Now we pump water for free during daylight hours.

🤝

Fair Labour Practice

Our workers get fair wages and proper rest. It's not just ethics — happy workers do better work. Simple as that.
